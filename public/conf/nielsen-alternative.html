Who Competes with NielsenIQ — and Where BioNixus Fits

Anyone typing “Nielsen alternative,” “NielsenIQ competitors,” or “companies like Nielsen” is usually buying one of these briefs: a brand or category team that already pays for NielsenIQ RMS and still cannot see named accounts, traditional trade, or SKU cuts; an OTC / consumer-health team that needs pharmacy and shopper primary beyond modern-trade panels; a pharma affiliate that needs HCP, payer, or hospital evidence NielsenIQ was never built to sell; or a mid-market team that cannot clear an enterprise retail-data contract for a single-country question.

How to brief
Keep NielsenIQ. Price the gap.

Five reasons commercial teams add primary research beside RMS — not instead of it.

1. Keep NielsenIQ. Price the gap.

NielsenIQ RMS is the right buy for national retail / FMCG / modern-trade measurement. Do not cancel it to “replace Nielsen.” Brief BioNixus for the account-level, SKU-level, or traditional-trade cut the feed cannot produce — then keep both on the insights stack.

2. Primary fieldwork, not another dashboard

BioNixus does not sell a second retail-measurement subscription. We field a scoped study: named accounts, SKUs, cities, and channels. The deliverable is primary evidence for a commercial decision, not another national share curve.

3. Traditional trade where volume still moves

In MENA and many emerging markets, bakals, wholesalers, and open markets still move volume that modern-trade panels undersample or exclude. Primary fieldwork is how you see that channel — not a dashboard filter NielsenIQ never sold you.

4. Healthcare depth NielsenIQ was not built for

OTC teams often already pay for NielsenIQ. Pharma and MedTech affiliates still need HCP, payer, KOL, pharmacy, and hospital evidence. That is a different product. Keep RMS for the consumer-health shelf; brief BioNixus for the clinical and access cut. For syndicated healthcare audits, see the IQVIA alternative.

5. No enterprise minimum

NielsenIQ is structured around syndicated retail-data contracts. BioNixus prices by project and country — typical 2026 bands $10,000–$75,000 USD single-country and $25,000–$120,000 USD multi-country. See pricing. You do not need an enterprise RMS minimum to ask one country, one brand, one account list.
