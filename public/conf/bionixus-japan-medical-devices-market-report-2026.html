Largest generic names: whole-body X-ray CT ¥88.1B (3.3%); discrete clinical-chemistry analysers ¥85.6B (3.2%); dental Au-Ag-Pd alloy ¥79.9B.

The dual structure is the commercial fact. Endoscopes are 9.9% of production and 22.7% of exports, but only 2.8% of domestic shipments. Blood-testing instruments are 8.5% of production and 15.9% of exports, but 1.3% of domestic shipments. The inverse is orthopaedics and catheters. A launch plan that treats imaging as the domestic P&L will mis-size the hospital wallet.

Industrial concentration

MHLW counts 1,725 device manufacturers in 2024. Forty-six firms with monthly production of ¥1 billion or more account for 64.5% of production value. Leading prefectures: Tochigi, Shizuoka, Ibaraki, Fukushima, Saitama, Aichi, Chiba.

Demography & Installed Base
Demand architecture.

Device volume in Japan is a function of age structure, bed density and near-universal NHI coverage — not of population growth. The population is already shrinking; the 75+ cohort is still rising.

01 · Population

Super-aged, still ageing at the top

On 1 October 2025 Japan had 123.22 million people. Those aged 65+ numbered 36.22 million (29.4%); those aged 75+ numbered 21.27 million (17.3%) and already outnumber the 65–74 group (14.95 million). The 2026 ageing white paper projects the 65+ share at 38.7% by 2070.

02 · Hospitals

Fewer facilities, still enormous capacity

The 2024 MHLW facility survey counted 8,060 hospitals (−62), 1,469,845 hospital beds (−11,338), 105,207 general clinics (+313) and 66,378 dental clinics. Medical corporations hold 56.3% of hospital beds. Occupancy 77.0%; average length of stay 25.6 days; daily inpatients 1,133,196.

03 · OECD

The densest imaging park in the club

12.5 hospital beds per 1,000 (OECD 4.2) and 184 CT, MRI and PET units per million (OECD 51). Practising physicians 2.6 per 1,000 (OECD 3.9). Health spend 10.6% of GDP. Life expectancy 84.1 years. 100% population coverage for a core set of services.

04 · Labour & disease

Care technology is policy

IDF Atlas 11th edition: adult (20–79) diabetes prevalence 8.1% in 2024. CS Japan records a projected shortfall of 570,000 caregivers by 2040. In June 2024 the government published Priority Areas for Caregiving Technology — transfer, mobility, toileting, bathing, monitoring, dementia.

Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices Agency
PMDA regulatory pathway.

MHLW issues the marketing authorisation. PMDA reviews the dossier. Classification into Class I–IV determines whether the file is a prefectural notification, a third-party certification, or a full PMDA/MHLW approval. Getting the class wrong is the most expensive unforced error in a Japan launch.

Class I

Todokede · 届出

Prefectural notification. No pre-market PMDA technical review. Fastest path when the JMDN code is correctly assigned.

Class II

Ninsho · 認証

Registered Certification Body against Japan Certification Standards mapped to JMDN. Only when a JCS exists for that code.

Class III / IV

Shonin · 承認

Full PMDA review and MHLW approval. Clinical evidence for novel high-risk technologies. This is still “device lag.”

Unmet need

Sakigake · 先駆け

Japan among earliest markets. Dedicated consultation; review target on the order of six months versus twelve.

Chuikyo, FAP & Hospital Conversion
NHI reimbursement & access sequence.

PMDA approval is not a sale. Almost all hospital procedures are reimbursed through the National Health Insurance fee schedule. Devices are priced by functional category, not by brand, and the Foreign Average Price rule compresses premiums on imported innovation.

01

Functional classification

Each NHI category carries a single reimbursement price. A differentiated product coded into a generic category inherits that ceiling. JMDN selection at filing is a pricing decision.

02

Listing windows — C1 / C2 and A / B / C

C1 and C2 are the premium tracks for genuine innovation. Missing the next Chuikyo window after MHLW approval can add months of approved-but-unreimbursed inventory.

03

Foreign Average Price (FAP) rule

Since 2002 Japan has referenced average list prices in the US, UK, France, Germany and Australia. CS Japan (Nov 2025) notes successive revisions have been used to reduce reimbursement on innovative imported devices.

04

DPC / PDPS and Advanced Medical Care

Inpatient economics sit inside a bundled daily payment. 先進医療 allows specified technologies at hospital cost while NHI evidence is collected — a bridge, not a commercial end-state.

Step 01

Consultation

PMDA 相談 six to nine months before file. Lock class, JMDN, bridging, route.

Step 02

MAH / QMS

Appoint the in-country holder. Confirm QMS Ordinance covers the overseas site.

Step 03

File the clock

FY2024 standard new-device total review: 11.7 months vs 12-month target.

Step 04

Chuikyo then PO

Sakigake shortens review; it does not move Chuikyo or hospital conversion.

Where a 2026–29 Plan Should Concentrate
Growth corridors & vendor triangulation.
Import-filled demand

Orthopaedics, catheters, structural heart

The three largest import classes — orthopaedics ¥523.7B, tubes/catheters ¥511.6B, physical-therapy ¥431.6B — are also among the largest domestic shipment classes. Addressable for well-prepared foreign OEMs if FAP and functional coding are modelled before the file.

SaMD

DASH for SaMD 2 is a live pathway

September 2023 MHLW programme and PMDA’s six-month priority-SaMD review goal. Two-stage approval is the practical mechanism. Software finally has a named track rather than a hardware-centric class.

Care technology

570,000 caregivers short by 2040

June 2024 priority-area policy is a demand map. It often sits in long-term-care insurance, not NHI hospital fees — adjacent to the device yearbook, not inside it.

Export platform

Japan as manufacturing base

43% of 2024 production value was exported (¥1.14T of ¥2.66T). Japanese OEMs entering GCC/MENA — Olympus, Terumo, Sysmex — are a separate BioNixus workstream.
