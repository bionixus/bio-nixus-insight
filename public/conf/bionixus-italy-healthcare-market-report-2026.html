03 · SSN architecture
National reimbursement is not regional access

Law 833/1978 created the service. Title V (2001) made Regions the operators.

That single sentence still explains most delayed Italian launches. GPs gatekeep. By 2023, 70% of GPs worked in group practice — which is why Italy records the EU’s lowest avoidable admissions for diabetes, CHF, asthma and COPD.

04 · Financing
Hospitals are covered. Families pay for the rest.

Italy spends less than peers and leaves outpatient care to households.

On OECD SHA in the Country Health Profile, 2023 CHE was 8.4% of GDP (EU 10.0%) and €3,086 PPP per capita (EU €3,832). Public sources covered just over 73%. Public financing covered 96% of hospital spend but only 58% of outpatient medical services. Health as a share of general-government spending: 12% in 2023, a historic low.

OOP is not the ticket

08 · Regulation & access
Fast to a national decision. Slow to a treated patient in some regions.

Plan AIFA and the 21 regional formularies as two clocks, not one.

EFPIA WAIT measures time to reimbursement — not uptake in Calabria. Mean time 439 days vs EU 578. Share reimbursed 83% (second only to Germany). Oncology 86% / 441 days. Orphans 70% vs EU 42%.

Waiting lists

11 · Implications
So what · for industry

Italy rewards companies that can live with hospital net prices, MEAs and payback.

The industrial base is a genuine EU asset. The SSN budget is not growing like the specialty pipeline. Primary research that stops at AIFA will mis-forecast uptake.

01 · Do

What the evidence supports

File AIFA with an MEA and registry plan on day one for oncology, immunology and rare disease. Model payback at ~50% of direct-purchase overrun. Sequence Lombardy, Emilia-Romagna, Veneto, Lazio. Put DPC/direct distribution in the forecast.

02 · Do not

Errors this edition is written to prevent

Do not add Farmindustria €74B to OsMed €37.2B. Do not quote €19B devices as SSN addressable. Do not treat FSN €136.5B as SHA spend. Do not use EFPIA WAIT days as time-to-first-patient in Calabria.

03 · Watch

Live policy clocks 2026–27

Testo Unico (opened July 2025). PNGLA platform and €100M/year from 2026. Innovative-status binary rules. Devices payback litigation. PNRR health spend closing in 2026.

04 · View

High outcome. Mid spend. Hospital net price.

The prize is inclusion in direct purchasing, a workable MEA, regional formulary speed in the four or five regions that also receive inbound mobility, and an honest after-payback P&L.
