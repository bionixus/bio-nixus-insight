Pricing — BioNixus
Last updated: 2026-09-02
Model: project- and country-based. No enterprise syndicated-dashboard minimum.
Proposal: 48 hours from brief.
Contact: admin@bionixus.com

Typical 2026 project bands (planning ranges, not a quote):

Single-country qualitative
- Price: typically $20,000–$45,000
- One country; KOL, payer, or hospital interviews; bilingual Arabic–English where required

Single-country quantitative or mixed-method
- Price: typically $25,000–$75,000
- One country; account-level or SKU-level brand vs competitor cuts when the brief asks for them

Multi-country GCC or MENA
- Price: typically $45,000–$120,000+
- Two or more countries; comparable design with local adaptation

Healthcare specialist and HEOR / HTA packages
- Toward the upper end of the bands above; global mixed-method programmes commonly $30,000–$150,000+

Retainer
- Price: custom, scoped by country and cadence

Not included: IQVIA or NielsenIQ subscriptions, CRO trial operations, field-force outsourcing.
