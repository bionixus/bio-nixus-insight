Pharmaceuticals

UK pharmaceutical market

FAQ size GBP 20–22 billion (hero ~£21B); KPI GBP 21–24 billion (ABPI). NHS is the primary payer. A positive NICE Technology Appraisal mandates NHS funding within 90 days in England.

NICE routes (live page)

STA for most single products. MTA when a class is compared. HST for ultra-rare disease (higher QALY threshold). Independent ERG critiques the submission. Manufacturers may appeal draft guidance. ICER typically GBP 20,000–30,000/QALY; end-of-life / severity modifier applies. Budget-impact test: projected cost above about GBP 20 million in any of the first three years can trigger extra NHS England commercial negotiation before the 90-day mandate.

MHRA after Brexit

National MA or reliance. IRP accepts FDA, EMA or TGA approvals — page access section: typical 60–150 days. ILAP (MHRA + NICE + NHSE + SMC) aligns regulatory and HTA clocks for unmet need. KPI standard review: 210 days; 70-day abridged for generic/biosimilar.

FAQ therapy ranking by NHS spend

Oncology (largest; Cancer Drugs Fund) · cardiovascular (statins, anticoagulants, antihypertensives) · diabetes (GLP-1 growing rapidly) · immunology/biologics · respiratory (COPD/asthma biologics, ICS/LABA). Mental-health spend is growing with prescribing rates.

KPI (ABHI)

UKCA marking is the Great Britain replacement for CE marking; the UK has repeatedly extended recognition of CE-marked devices. Northern Ireland continues to accept CE marking under the Windsor Framework — a dual-track requirement. 3,500+ medtech companies (FAQ).

NHS Supply Chain

Central procurement organisation for framework agreements and category towers (consumables, devices, clinical products) used by NHS trusts in England. A framework award is necessary for routine trust purchasing but does not guarantee uptake — trusts and ICBs still choose what to stock. Categories named on the page: orthopaedics, cardiovascular devices, wound care, diagnostics.

Private groups — HCA, Spire, Nuffield Health, Ramsay — buy outside NHS Supply Chain (~10,000 private beds vs ~141,000 NHS).

12–24 mo
Regulatory approval
12–18 mo

27–54 mo
Launch to access

Oncology CDF can shorten the total clock to about 24 months (KPI). STA 12–18 months; MTA 18–24 months.

1
MHRA MA — national or IRP (FDA/EMA/TGA). Devices: UKCA / CE (extended recognition) + Windsor Framework in NI.
2
NICE dossier — can run in parallel post-MA or via managed access.
3
Appraisal Committee — 3–6 meetings over 12–18 months. ERG critique. Appeal window.
4
NHS England commercial framework — 3–6 months post-NICE. VPAG (2024–2028) repayments above the growth allowance; statutory scheme if a company does not join. FAQ pharma-size question still refers to the predecessor name VPAS.
5
Cancer Drugs Fund — conditional oncology access while evidence is collected; then NICE reassesses for routine commissioning.
6
ICB formulary adoption — 42 ICBs; 3–12 months. Postcode variation for speed and for products outside the statutory TA mandate.

Devolution

Four nations and ICBs

Nation / bodyPublished access fact
England — 42 ICBsReplaced CCGs (Health and Care Act 2022). Implement NICE TAs; retain discretion on local formulary speed and on products outside the statutory funding direction.
Scotland — SMCOwn HTA; typically advice within ~12–18 weeks of MA. PACE process for end-of-life and orphan medicines.
Wales — AWMSGAppraises medicines NICE has not reviewed or that sit outside NICE remit. NICE TAs generally mandated for NHS Wales within a set window.
Northern IrelandDepartment of Health typically endorses NICE TAs through its own adoption process — not a parallel appraisal body.
NHS England Specialised CommissioningHigh-cost, low-volume (gene therapy, rare disease, complex devices) — national commercial negotiation alongside NICE.
Do not treat a single England NICE TA as a UK-wide listing date. Sequence SMC, AWMSG and NI adoption as separate workstreams.
