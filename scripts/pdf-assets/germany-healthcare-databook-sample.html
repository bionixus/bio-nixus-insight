Pharmaceuticals

German pharmaceutical market

Published size: FAQ EUR 52–58 billion (hero ~€55B); KPI EUR 53–57 billion (vfa/ABDA). Largest pharmaceutical market in the EU. GKV is about 65% of pharmaceutical spend.

AMNOG in one page

AMNOG (2011) requires a benefit dossier within three months of launch. IQWiG assesses evidence against the G-BA’s zweckmäßige Vergleichstherapie (ZVT), focusing on mortality, morbidity and quality of life. G-BA assigns one of six ratings: major / considerable / minor / non-quantifiable / no additional benefit / less benefit. The rating drives the GKV-Spitzenverband price negotiation (usually within 12 months). No proven added benefit → Festbetrag reference group. Orphan drugs: modified path; KPI notes exemption up to EUR 50M annual GKV revenue.

Free pricing at launch for 12 months makes Germany a preferred first-launch market in Europe. The negotiated (or arbitrated) price then applies from month 13, with a retroactive rebate on months 1–12 at list price (KPI path).

FAQ therapy ranking by GKV spend

Oncology (checkpoint inhibitors, ADCs, CAR-T) · immunology and biologics (TNF, IL-17/23, JAK; biosimilar substitution expanding) · cardiovascular (high-volume statins, anticoagulants, heart-failure) · diabetes (GLP-1, SGLT-2) · CNS/neurology (MS DMTs, Alzheimer, antidepressants). Gene and cell therapy are emerging high-value categories.

KPI (BVMed)

Largest medical-devices market in the EU. Germany is both a major consumer and manufacturer — Siemens Healthineers, B. Braun, Karl Storz, Dräger, Fresenius Medical Care (FAQ). MDR 2017/745 is mandatory. BfArM plus EU notified bodies (KPI).

Hospital funding of innovation

InEK updates the DRG catalogue annually. High-cost innovation can sit in a funding gap until the tariff catches up. NUB (Neue Untersuchungs- und Behandlungsmethoden) lets hospitals apply for a temporary supplementary payment for novel methods — a common early step for device manufacturers.

Access clock

AMNOG sequence

12–24 mo
Regulatory approval

Free launch / list price

Negotiated price in effect
1
EMA CAP or BfArM MRP/DCP — 210-day EMA standard. PEI for biologics.
2
AMNOG dossier to G-BA — Day 0, mandatory with commercial launch (KPI); FAQ: within 3 months of launch. Modules 1–5; patient-relevant endpoints.
3
IQWiG benefit assessment — 3 months post-dossier (KPI); published reports typically ~12 weeks after submission (FAQ).
4
G-BA resolution — 6 months post-launch. Six-tier Zusatznutzen. Hearings with manufacturer and independent experts.
5
GKV-Spitzenverband negotiation — months 7–12. International price comparisons matter.
6
Schiedsstelle — if no deal in the statutory window (typically 6 months after G-BA). Binding price in 3 months. Appeals to social courts are rare and slow.
7
Regional KV + hospital formularies — no formal regional HTA; KV incentive schemes still move volume.
