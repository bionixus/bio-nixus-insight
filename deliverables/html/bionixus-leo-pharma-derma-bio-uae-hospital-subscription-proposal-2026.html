01 — The offer

One fee per hospital,
five products inside it.

This quotation is for a subscription to hospital consumption data for the LEO Pharma dermatology biologic franchise in the United Arab Emirates. The price is per hospital and per reporting cycle. The five products sit inside that fee. They are not charged again.

Sixteen hospitals are named below. Seven take the flagship rate: Dubai Hospital and Rashid Hospital (DHA), Tawam Hospital, Sheikh Shakhbout Medical City and Sheikh Khalifa Medical City (SEHA), Cleveland Clinic Abu Dhabi, and American Hospital Dubai. The other nine take the standard rate.

The brief also counts eleven standard-tier hospitals. Nine of those are named in this document. Two places stay open at the standard unit fee. They are not in the totals until LEO Pharma names them.

Each hospital is bought at one depth. Hospital, department, or indication. The three packages assume the same depth across the whole panel. A mixed panel is priced from the same rate card, hospital by hospital.

What each cycle contains. A hospital-by-product table for the five named products, the cut that matches the depth purchased, movement against the prior cycle, and a source note (system, period, unit). The five product names are fixed at kick-off and held for the term. A change of product is a variation.

06 — Terms

How the subscription runs.

Cadence

Quarterly is the subscription. One reporting cycle each quarter, invoiced at the list fee at the start of the quarter.

Monthly option

A monthly subscription is the same scope, delivered each month, at the list fee less 10% for that cycle. It is chosen at the start and held for the term unless both parties agree a change.

Currency and tax

All figures are US dollars and exclusive of VAT. Where the invoice is raised in the UAE, VAT at the prevailing rate is added.

Access

A hospital is invoiced for a cycle only when the file for that cycle is delivered. If a named hospital cannot be obtained, BioNixus proposes a replacement in the same tier at the same fee, or drops that hospital from the cycle.

Products

The five products are written into the kick-off note and stay fixed. Adding or replacing a product is quoted before the next cycle.

Use

LEO Pharma may use the files internally for commercial, medical, and market-access decisions on this franchise. The files are not for resale or for publication outside LEO Pharma.

Term

The subscription continues until cancelled. Either party may cancel with 30 days’ written notice before the next cycle. Cycles already invoiced are delivered.

Validity

This quotation is valid for 30 days from 1 October 2026. Acceptance is by signature below, with the depth and the cadence marked.

To start

Mark the depth, mark quarterly or monthly, and name the five products. If two further standard-tier hospitals belong on the panel, name them before signature so they enter the first invoice. Otherwise the first invoice is the sixteen-hospital fee on page 4.
