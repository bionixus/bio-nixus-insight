02 — Access model

Three doors into the same hospital.

Each answers a different commercial question. Together they show demand, what the pharmacy actually issued, and who wrote the order.

01

Physicians

The name on the order line. Useful where influence and volume are not the same person.

  • Ordering physician per line
  • Strength, quantity, unit, sale date
  • Which brands a named clinician moves
02

Pharmacists

The issue file. What left the store, not what a panel model allocated to the country.

  • Requested versus issued quantity
  • Month, issuing point, value in local currency
  • Where demand was only partly filled
03

Buyers

The procurement seat: formulary, tender, and the decision to keep stocking the brand.

  • Public hospital and private account routes
  • Who controls the buy, not only the script
  • Why an issue file moved, or stopped

04 — How an engagement runs

Five steps, one deliverable you can defend.

1

Scope the decision

Brand, country, hospitals that matter, and the grain required — hospital, indication, or physician.

2

Secure the account files

Issue and request files through the pharmacy and buyer routes, in the format the hospital system produces.

3

Field the physician layer

Ordering-physician records where available; structured interviews or surveys to confirm use and line of therapy.

4

Reconcile and cut

Hospital, product, indication, and physician views reconciled to the source, with request-versus-issue flagged.

5

Deliver for action

Account ranking, demand-versus-supply gaps, named-physician view, and the indication cut where commissioned.

Compliance posture

Market research, clearly distinguished from clinical research. GDPR-compliant data handling; alignment with EMA, MHRA, SFDA, and Ministry of Health frameworks; ICH-GCP awareness; NHS REC standards. Physician identities and account values are handled under client-specific confidentiality terms.

Therapy-area depth relevant here

  • Immunology — dermatology, rheumatology, gastroenterology
  • Dermatology — atopic dermatitis, psoriasis
  • Respiratory — asthma, COPD, IPF
  • Biologics and biosimilars — landscape, switching, competitive intelligence
