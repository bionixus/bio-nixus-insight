### Post 2 — How we field a pharmacy mystery shop

A pharmacy mystery shopper walks named outlets and records stock, facing, price, and substitution. Syndicated listing says “available.” The store may not.

This is not the GCC retail grocery brief. It is pharmacy and pharmaceutical availability.

Request a pharmacy mystery-shop proposal: https://www.bionixus.com/pharmacy-mystery-shopper

### Post 3 — How to brief an HCP ATU in 20 minutes

An HCP ATU (awareness, trial, usage) is the default physician tracker. IQVIA reports what was dispensed. An ATU asks whether the specialist has tried the brand — and why not.

Send: country, specialty, wave timing, and the decision the affiliate has to make. We return a proposal in 48 hours.

Brief an HCP ATU: https://www.bionixus.com/hcp-atu-study
