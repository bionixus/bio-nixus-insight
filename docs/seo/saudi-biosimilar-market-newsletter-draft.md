## Body

One number keeps showing up in Saudi biosimilar board packs: “the market is X hundred million.”

Then another deck cites a different X.

BioNixus sizes the **hospital-administered** Saudi Arabia biosimilar market at roughly **USD 382 million in 2026** (~15% CAGR toward ~USD 668M by 2030). Broader published estimates (for example IMARC at USD 664.2M in 2025) include a wider molecule and channel set. Insights10’s mAb-only trajectory sits in between.

That is not a data fight. It is a **scope** choice.

- Planning NUPCO immunology / oncology frameworks → use the hospital band.  
- Building an all-channel total → cite the broader figure **and** footnote the hospital core.
